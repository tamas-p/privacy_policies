Privacy Policy for mot

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how mot ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information Stored Locally

  • Stopwatch & Timing Data: Your active stopwatch logs, lap times, and timing sessions are processed directly on your device to enable core app functionality.

B. Information Stored via Firebase Services

To save your progress and sync exercise records, mot uses Google Firebase (Firebase Realtime Database / Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, if applicable):

  • Exercise Records: Dates, durations, and timestamps of your exercise sessions.
  • Account Identifiers: If you log in, we store a unique user identifier (UID) provided by Firebase Authentication.
  • App Performance & Diagnostic Data: If Firebase Crashlytics or Analytics is enabled, anonymous technical data (such as app crash logs, device model, and OS version) may be collected automatically to maintain app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Track and display your exercise history and stopwatch sessions.
  • Sync your data across sessions or devices via Firebase.
  • Maintain, improve, and troubleshoot app performance.

3. Third-Party Services

mot relies on Google Firebase to manage database storage and analytics. Google Firebase processes data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Security & Privacy

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your exercise dates and stopwatch logs remain in Firebase for as long as you maintain your account or use the app.
  • You may request the deletion of your stored exercise records or account data at any time by contacting us at the email provided below or using any in-app deletion options.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information stored in Firebase by utilizing secure authentication rules and standard encryption protocols in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

mot does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app or on our policy page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
